I am an Electrical Engineering student at [École Polytechnique d'Abomey-Calavi (EPAC)](https://epac-uac.bj/), Benin, and a research student at the Bénin Excellence AI Lab under the supervision of Johannes Hounsinou. My core interests lie in learning-driven robotic systems, with a main goal to deploy intelligent autonomy into the physical world. I emphasize embodied AI models, real-world reinforcement learning, and novel robotic system design and control. I aim to dedicate my research career to building robots with brains that learn to inhabit and act in the physical world, drawing from the same principles that govern living systems.
I believe the future of intelligence is physical. Reach out if you think so too (:
