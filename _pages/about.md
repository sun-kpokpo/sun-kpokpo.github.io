I am a third-year Electrical Engineering student at [École Polytechnique d'Abomey-Calavi (EPAC)](https://epac-uac.bj/), Benin, specializing in Industrial Process Control. I am also a research student at the Bénin Excellence AI Lab, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Johannes Hounsinou.

My research interests center on robot learning, especially sim-to-real reinforcement learning for locomotion and manipulation in resource-constrained settings. I am drawn to the question of how robots can learn useful physical skills when hardware, sensing, compute, and institutional resources are limited. So far, much of my path in robotics has been self-directed, built alongside my formal training in electrical engineering.

More broadly, my core interests lie in learning-driven robotic systems, with the long-term goal of deploying intelligent autonomy into the physical world. I emphasize embodied AI models, real-world reinforcement learning, and novel robotic system design and control. I want to dedicate my research career to building robots with brains that learn to inhabit and act in the physical world, drawing from principles that govern living systems. I believe the future of intelligence is physical.

My current research project investigates whether servo position error can serve as a pseudo-tactile signal for grasp-state disambiguation on low-cost, position-only gripper hardware. The goal is to make contact-aware manipulation more accessible on platforms that do not expose force, torque, or current feedback, and to open a path toward sim-to-real manipulation policies on constrained robots.

Outside the lab, I contribute to robotics projects through [MA64 Robotics Association]({{ '/contributions/' | relative_url }}), including ROS2 navigation work on Robot Indoor and Faucon MA64. I am also involved in Sirius Space Association, where we help make space science more visible and approachable in Benin, and I serve as Student Clubs Coordinator for electronics, industrial computing, and robotics in my department's student association.

I am currently looking for research internships, collaborations, and graduate programs where I can deepen the theory of robotics, control, and learning while continuing to build and test real systems. I am especially interested in environments that combine rigorous research with hands-on robotics.
